Neon Bay 3D is an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built as plain static files with three.js (vendored locally, no network requests): explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Bay PD through a 5-star wanted system with pursuit cars, RAM/PIT interceptors, a NEON-1 helicopter with a searchlight, roadblocks and SWAT, and run ten story missions for Vic and Lena across four districts with their own standing.

The city has a downtown of towers, a beachfront, a working docks district, the Hills, and a causeway bridge with stunt ramps. Systems include third-person on-foot and driving, traffic AI, fleeing pedestrians, four weapon tiers (fists, pistol, shotgun, SMG) with a weapon wheel, a garage with engine/armor/paint upgrades, crew abilities (Vic's free repairs, Lena's cash-drop spotter), respect ranks, a 3-station synth radio, collectible cash drops, burning and exploding cars, a day-to-dusk sky, and save/load — all rendered in 3D with synthesised WebAudio sound.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Controls: WASD move / drive · mouse drag orbits the camera · E enter / steal car · click punch / shoot · 1-4 / Q weapons · Tab weapon wheel · R radio · T next station · G garage (near Vic's) · M next mission · P pause.
